# Tracking your request (citizens)

## Finding your booking

- Open **Textile Collection → My requests** and look for your **`DLN-`** reference.
- Each request shows its current stage on a progress bar.

## What each stage means

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending review | Sent. A staff member will check it. |
| Ready to group | Approved. Waiting to be placed on a trip. |
| Scheduled | On a trip. Your page shows the date and time window. |
| Collected / Received | Done. Shows confirmed bags and weight. |
| Missed | The driver could not collect. It can be rescheduled. |
| Rejected | Not accepted, with the reason shown. |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn. |

## Rescheduling

- Open the request and choose a new date before the cutoff shown on the page.
- If your slot became unavailable, the page shows the next open date — or switch to drop-off, which needs no slot.

## If something looks wrong

- Share your `DLN-` reference with support — it identifies the exact booking.
- Rejection and cancellation reasons are always shown on the request itself.
