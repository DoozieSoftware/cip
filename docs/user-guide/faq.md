# Quick answers

- **What do I need for a home pickup?** At least 2 bags or 4 kg.
- **I have only 1 bag. What now?** Use centre drop-off — any amount is accepted.
- **Is 2 light bags enough?** Yes — meeting the bag count alone passes.
- **Is 4 kg in one bag enough?** Yes — meeting the weight alone passes.
- **Why is my submit button disabled?** The estimate is below both minimums, or a required field is incomplete. The warning on the page names the exact problem.
- **Can staff approve my small pickup anyway?** No. Small loads go to drop-off.
- **What do `DLN-` and `DRL-` mean?** `DLN-` is your booking; `DRL-` is the driver’s trip.
- **My slot is unavailable. Now what?** Pick the suggested next open date, or switch to drop-off.
- **My pickup was missed. What happens?** The reason is shown on your request and it can go on a later trip.
- **Why was my request rejected?** The reason is always shown on the request itself.
- **Who do I contact?** Share your `DLN-` reference with support.
- **Does the driver see my phone number?** The driver can contact you for the pickup; staff private numbers are never shown to citizens.
- **What if the driver has no signal?** The work saves on the device and syncs later — nothing is lost.
