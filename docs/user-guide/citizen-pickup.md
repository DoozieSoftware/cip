# Requesting a home pickup (citizens)

> **Where to find it:** sidebar **Collections** → **New request**
> (`/citizen/textile-collections/new`)

## Before you start

Have ready: your name, phone number, email, pickup address, and a rough idea of how much you have — bag count or weight. Either one is enough; you do not need a weighing machine.

## Step by step

1. Open **Textile Collection → New request**.
2. Choose the material:
   - **Clothes and wearable items** — wearable or torn, both accepted.
   - **Metal scrap** — separate from other waste.
   - **E-waste** — batteries, cables, small appliances.
3. Choose your service zone (Kengeri, Jayanagar, or Whitefield).
4. Choose **“Pick up from my home.”**
5. Fill in your name, phone (for pickup updates), email (for the receipt), and full pickup address with house or flat number, street, and a landmark.
6. Enter **how many bags** or **about how many kg**. One sack counts as one bag.
7. Optionally add a photo of your bags — one clear daylight photo is enough, max 10 MB. You can also add it later.
8. Give the request a short title, for example “2 bags of old clothes”.
9. Press **Send pickup request**.

## The minimum check

- The page shows what your zone needs: **2 bags or 4 kg**.
- As soon as your estimate falls below both, a warning appears: *“Cannot submit — below the pickup minimum.”* The submit button reads **Pickup minimum not met** and stays disabled.
- Fix it by adding more bags, or switch to **centre drop-off**, which accepts any amount.

## What happens next

- Your booking gets a **`DLN-`** reference. Save it.
- A staff member reviews the request.
- Once scheduled, your page shows the pickup date and time window.
- On the day, keep bags packed and accessible. The driver records the actual bags and weight with a proof photo.
- If the pickup is missed, the request can be rescheduled — see [Tracking your request](tracking.md).

## Tips for a smooth pickup

- Keep textiles dry and packed in bags.
- Leave bags where the crew can reach them without calling you.
- If your phone number changes, update it on the request so the driver can reach you.
