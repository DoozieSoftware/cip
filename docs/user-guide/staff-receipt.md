# Confirming centre receipts (staff)

> **Where to find it:** sidebar **Receipt**
> (`/operations/textile-collections/receipt`)

For citizens who bring bags to the centre themselves.

## Step by step

1. Open the **Receipt** screen.
2. Find the booking by `DLN-` reference or the citizen’s phone number.
3. Verify the citizen’s identity and material.
4. Weigh the bags and enter the **actual bags and kg**.
5. Take the required **proof photo**.
6. Press **Confirm receipt**. The citizen’s page updates to **received** with the confirmed quantity.

## Notes

- Any amount is accepted — there is no minimum at the counter.
- Receipts are recorded per booking with staff, time, and photo for the audit trail.
- A drop-off booking is never completed from the driver flow, and a doorstep pickup is never completed from the counter flow.
