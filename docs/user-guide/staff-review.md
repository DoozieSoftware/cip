# Reviewing requests (staff)

> **Where to find it:** sidebar **Reviews**
> (`/operations/textile-collections/review`); tap a request for its detail page

## Opening the queue

Log in as the department officer and open **Reviews**. New citizen bookings arrive with status **pending review**, newest first. Use search, zone, and category filters to work through the list.

## What to check on each request

1. **Material and zone** — is it something Dr. Linen collects in that zone?
2. **Quantity** — home pickups below 2 bags and 4 kg cannot be approved as pickup; ask the citizen to add more or switch to drop-off.
3. **Address** — complete enough for a driver to find on first try?
4. **Photo** — citizen evidence photo, if attached.
5. **Method** — pickup requests go toward trips; drop-off bookings go to the **Receipt** screen, never to a trip.

## Actions

- **Approve** — a pickup becomes *ready to group* (scheduling pool); a drop-off becomes *awaiting drop* with the centre details sent to the citizen.
- **Reject** — always include the reason. It is shown to the citizen; silent rejections are not allowed.
- **Cancel** — for duplicates or citizen-withdrawn requests, with a recorded reason.

## After review

- Approved pickups: see [Planning and scheduling trips](staff-scheduling.md).
- Approved drop-offs: see [Confirming centre receipts](staff-receipt.md).
